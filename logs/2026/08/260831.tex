\subsection*{$\mathcal{YD}$ of $\mathcal{YD}$ is $\mathcal{YD}$}

Let a braided group be a braided Hopf algebra in a braided category.
\begin{proposition}[\cite{Bespalov97}]
    Let $A$ be a braided gropu in $\mathcal{C}$ and $B$ be a braided group in $\mathcal{YD}(\mathcal{C})^A_A$.
    Then $(\mathcal{YD}(\mathcal{C})^A_A)^B_B \cong \mathcal{YD}(\mathcal{C})^{A \ltimes B}_{A \ltimes B}$.
\end{proposition}


\subsection*{Finite dual of the quantum double, continued}

Let $\{ e_i \}$ be a basis of $H$ and suppose that the dual linear functional $\{ e^i \}$ lies in $H^\circ$.
Then, one might say that a universal $\mathcal{R}$-matrix of the quantum double $D(H)$ is
\[
\mathcal{R} = \sum \jmath(e^i) \otimes \imath(e_i)
\]
where $\imath: H \hookrightarrow D(H)$ and $\jmath: H^{\circ, \text{op}} \hookrightarrow D(H)$ are inclusions.

Note that, for $g \in D(H)^\circ$, we have
\[
\imath^\circ(g) = \langle g, \imath(e_i) \rangle e^i
\]
by putting $e_i$ on the left-hand side.
From this, we have
\begin{align*}
\langle f \otimes g, \mathcal{R} \rangle & = \sum \langle f, \jmath(e^i)\rangle \langle g, \imath(e_i) \rangle \\
& = \sum \langle f, \jmath \left( \langle g, \imath(e_i) \rangle e^i \right) \rangle \\
& = \langle f, \jmath \left( \imath^\circ (g) \right) \rangle.
\end{align*}

Hence, a bilinear form
\[
\mathcal{R}: D(H)^\circ \otimes D(H)^\circ \rightarrow k, \quad f \otimes g \mapsto \langle f, (\jmath \circ \imath^\circ)(g) \rangle
\]
may generalize the univeral $\mathcal{R}$-matrix of $D(H)$ and make $D(H)^\circ$ dual quasitriangular.
